Abstract

   A central question when planning rapid transit systems is the determination of alignments and stations. Operational
research methods can help solve these problems and they are also useful for the assessment of the network charac-
teristics. This survey article reviews the main available methods.

Keywords: Rapid transit systems; Alignments; Stations; Location
